Hide in Plain Sight

Secrets, drawn in the open.

No key connected

Type a secret word. A Pollinations model draws you an ordinary picture, a house, an elephant, a row of quiet things, and hides the word inside it: the objects, read in order, start with its letters. Send the picture anywhere. To read it back, hand the picture code to a fresh model that has never seen your word and ask what is hidden.

Inspired by the post How does Fable 5.1 hide messages in images? The hiding here is an acrostic, simple and checkable: the app itself verifies that the drawn objects really spell your word.

Bring your own Pollen

Every model call runs on your own Pollinations account. Two ways to connect:

  1. Paste your key. Get one at enter.pollinations.ai/keys and paste it below. It is stored only in this browser (localStorage), sent only to gen.pollinations.ai, and you can wipe it any time.
  2. One click connect. If you have an app key (pk_..., created under Apps on the same page), paste it too and use the connect button. Pollinations then issues a scoped key for this app, with a budget and an expiry you approve. That key stays in session storage only.

How the trick works

The model picks one ordinary object per letter of your word: house, elephant, lion, lamp, owl for HELLO. It draws them in a row inside an otherwise normal scene. Nothing in the picture is a letter, so it survives a casual look, a screenshot, a repost. The reader, human or model, has to notice the row and read the initials. The app checks the spelling itself before it shows you the picture, and asks the model to redraw when the row spells the wrong word.

Honest limits: this is a toy lock, not encryption. Anyone who suspects a hidden word and reads the initials gets it. Use it for games, gifts, puzzles and quiet jokes, not for anything that must stay secret.
